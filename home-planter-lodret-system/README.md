# Planter LODRET system

Brynhild self-watering planters in 16 boho designs and several sizes, hanging on one LODRET vertical-rod system mounted on a 7/8" wooden dowel.

> **Credit: original design by [Epiq3D](https://makerworld.com/en/@EPIQ3D).**
> This whole system is derived from two of Epiq3D's models on MakerWorld:
> - [**Brynhild – Hanging planter (LODRET compatible)**](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1343487) (the self-watering pot, drip tray and wick feet)
> - [**LODRET system – for hanging planters**](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1343541) (wall rod mounts, rod hangers and clamp knobs)
>
> Everything here is a modification of that work: the pots are rescaled and re-surfaced but keep Epiq3D's original rail, drip-tray fit and wick system, and the LODRET parts are Epiq3D's with the rod bore opened to 22.6 mm. All credit for the original pot, drip-tray and LODRET designs goes to Epiq3D. Please download and support the originals on MakerWorld.
>
> Both originals are under MakerWorld's **Standard Digital File License**, which doesn't allow sharing or hosting the files or derivatives elsewhere. So **this repo contains no 3D model files**: no originals and no remixed print files. It has the documentation, renders and the scripts that do the remixing. To print, download Epiq3D's originals from MakerWorld yourself and generate the files you want (see [Generate the print files](#generate-the-print-files)).

## Rod (dowel) specification

| | |
|---|---|
| Rod used | [Pine Round Dowel, 48 in × 0.875 in, sanded](https://www.homedepot.com/pep/Pine-Round-Dowel-48-in-x-0-875-in-Sanded-and-Ready-for-Finishing-Versatile-Wooden-Rod-for-DIY-Home-Projects-6214U/329566303) (Home Depot, model 6214U) |
| Material | Pine (softwood). Snug the clamp knobs firmly but don't crank them, or they'll dent the wood. |
| Diameter | **7/8" = 22.2 mm** |
| Bore in hangers and wall mounts | **22.6 mm** (opened up from the original 22.2 mm so the dowel slides in; the knob clamps it) |
| Usable diameter range | about 20–22.6 mm (the clamp knob takes up the slack; the designer quotes "down to about 20 mm") |
| Orientation | **Vertical**: one wall mount at the top of the rod, one at the bottom, pots stacked along it |
| Length | 48 in (1219 mm) as bought, the same as the designer's 1.2 m rod for 3 planters. Use it full length to leave room for a third pot, or cut it to 0.8–1.0 m for just these two. |
| Finish | Pine soaks up water, so seal it (polyurethane, spar varnish or Danish oil) before mounting, at least where pots get watered. |

Thinner rods: LODRET also offers 14–18 mm and 19 mm (3/4") adapters on its MakerWorld page. They're not needed for a 7/8" dowel.

## Wall fixing

| | |
|---|---|
| Wall mounts | 2 (top and bottom of the rod), one screw each |
| Screw | shank **up to 4.5 mm** (4 mm / #8 works), head **up to 10 mm** across |
| Anchoring | Screw into a stud, or use wall anchors rated for the load (see weight below). Plain drywall is not enough. |

## The pots

Both pots keep the **original 100% dovetail rail** on their back, so each slides into a standard LODRET rod hanger. Only the pot body, drip tray and wick feet are scaled.

The joint matches the original exactly, measured from the rail top:

| | Original | 105% | 155% |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rail head length | 46.19 mm | 46.19 mm | 46.19 mm |
| Shoulder the hanger face rests on | 4.00 mm down | 4.00 mm | 4.00 mm |
| Pot surface under the hanger wings | 7.85 mm down | 7.85 mm | 7.85 mm |

The rib pattern runs right up to the rail, as on the original. The scaled pot's oversized rail was cut out and the gap refilled with the pot's own pattern: the ribs repeat every 20°, so a section rotated 180° lines up to within 0.005 mm. See `Previews/rail-joint-vs-original.png` and `08_pot155_back_rail.jpg`.

| | Original (100%) | **105% pot** | **155% pot** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening (inside rim) | 87 mm (3.4") | ~91 mm (3.6") | ~135 mm (5.3") |
| Inside depth | ~90 mm | ~94 mm | ~140 mm (5.5") |
| Widest inside | ~103 mm | ~108 mm | ~160 mm |
| Outside: front-to-back incl. rail × side-to-side × height | 114 × 107 × 93 mm | 119 × 112 × 98 mm | 173 × 166 × 144 mm |
| Soil volume | ~0.68 L | ~0.8 L | ~2.5 L |
| Drip tray | 100 ml | ~115 ml | ~370 ml |
| Fits | small herb pot | 8 cm nursery pot | 32 oz Fage tub (rim ~117–129 mm, ~124 mm tall) |

### Spacing on the rod

How far each pot reaches from the centre of its rail:

| Pot | Below rail centre | Above rail centre |
|---|---|---|
| 105% | ~42 mm | ~56 mm |
| 155% | ~62 mm | ~82 mm |

Minimum distance between two hangers = (lower pot's "above") + (upper pot's "below") + the height of the plant in the lower pot.
- 155% at the bottom, 105% above: **≥ ~120 mm + plant**. This is the recommended layout: the heavy pot sits near the bottom mount.
- 105% at the bottom, 155% above: ≥ ~125 mm + plant.

### Weight

A watered 155% pot is roughly 2.5–3 kg and a 105% pot roughly 1 kg. The whole setup is about 3.5–4 kg on the two wall screws.

## Print settings

Based on the designer's profile: 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% grid infill, supports on, PLA.

| Part | Settings |
|---|---|
| Pots | 0.2 mm, **4 walls**, 15% infill, supports **on (build plate only)** for the rail's underside. Print upright, opening up, as laid out in the files. |
| Rod hangers, knobs, wall mounts | **PETG** if available (else PLA), **5–6 walls, 40%+ infill** |
| Drip trays, wick feet | Default |

The generated pot STLs are large (100–220 MB) because of the surface pattern. They load slowly but slice fine; `build_sizes.py` writes compressed 3MFs instead.

## Assembly

1. Screw the two wall rod mounts to the wall, one above the other, with their bores lined up vertically.
2. Slide the dowel through both and tighten their knobs.
3. Slide one rod hanger per pot onto the dowel, set the spacing (see above), and tighten each knob.
4. Slide each pot's back rail into its hanger's channel.
5. Fit the 3 wick feet and the drip tray as in the original Brynhild design. Use a wicking layer (e.g. cotton) in the bottom of the pot.

## Generate the print files

You need Epiq3D's two originals, Python 3.10+ with `numpy`, `scipy`, `trimesh` and `manifold3d` (`pip install numpy scipy trimesh manifold3d`), and Blender 4.2+ for the renders only.

1. **Download the originals** from MakerWorld (free account): [Brynhild – Hanging planter](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1343487) and [LODRET system](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1343541). Save them as `Original downloads/Brynhild_hanging_planter.3mf` and `Original downloads/LODRET_system.3mf`.
2. **Set up a work folder** (`<work>` below) and pull the parts out as STL:
   ```bash
   mkdir -p <work>/in
   cp "Original downloads/"*.3mf <work>/in/
   python "Source scripts/extract3mf.py" <work>/in/Brynhild_hanging_planter.3mf <work>/planter
   python "Source scripts/extract3mf.py" <work>/in/LODRET_system.3mf <work>/lodret
   ```
3. **LODRET parts with the 22.6 mm bore:** `python "Source scripts/build.py" <work> <out>` (use only its `LODRET_*` files; it also writes an early 155% pot that `build_pot.py` replaces)
4. **Stock spiral pot at any size** (pot + drip tray + feet as STL): `python "Source scripts/build_pot.py" <work> <out> 1.05`
5. **Boho designs:** make the 100% textured pots and trays, then build print sets (pot with rail + tray + 3 feet, one 3MF each) at any sizes:
   ```bash
   python "Source scripts/boho_designs.py" <work> <work>/designs                 # all designs, or name some: rattan mudcloth
   python "Source scripts/build_sizes.py" <work> <work>/designs <out> 105,125,155 rattan mountains
   ```
   Design names: `original rattan rope reeded dunes mudcloth berber petals herringbone mountains arches slats sunburst ripples fern triptych`.
6. **Check a pot** (optional): `python "Source scripts/check_joint.py" <work> joint.png <pot.stl>` compares its rail joint with the original's.

Every file these scripts make keeps Epiq3D's interior, rail, drip-tray fit and wick system, so it stays a derivative of their work. Keep it for personal use, under the original license.

### How the remix works

- **Rail at 100%.** A scaled pot keeps no rail of its own. For textured designs the new skin covers the back; for the stock spiral the oversized rail is cut out and refilled from the pot turned 180° (the spiral repeats every 20°). The original rail is then added on a column shaped like its base, seated so the joint matches the original exactly (rail head 46.19 mm, shoulder 4.00 mm, wing clearance 7.85 mm).
- **Textures.** `boho_designs.py` shaves the pot and tray down to the smooth surface under the ribs and adds a 1.2–1.6 mm height-field skin that continues across the pot/tray joint and fades out under the rim.
- **Drip tray fit.** The pot's bottom and the groove the tray snaps into are centred on the pot's axis, 3.23 mm (×scale) behind the centre of its bounding box. The tray centres on that axis, and its 54 spiral ribs continue the pot's (`tray_alignment.py` measures this). It only matters for the renders.

To redo the renders: `assemble.py` works out where every part sits, then `render_blender.py` renders the system, and `render_designs.py` renders the design options (both with `blender -b -P <script> -- …`; usage at the top of each script).
